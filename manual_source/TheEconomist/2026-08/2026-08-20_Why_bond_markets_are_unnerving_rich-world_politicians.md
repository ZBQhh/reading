# Why bond markets are unnerving rich-world politicians

> **作者**: The Economist | **发布日期**: 2026-08-20 | **来源**: [TheEconomist](https://www.economist.com/finance-and-economics/2026/08/19/why-bond-markets-are-unnerving-rich-world-politicians)

---

Global stockmarkets are booming, but government bond yields across the developed world tell a far more cautious and unsettling story.

Long-term sovereign yields have climbed steadily, reaching levels unseen since before the 2008 financial crisis. From Washington to London and Tokyo, politicians find themselves increasingly constrained by the demands of creditors.

The central driver is the sheer scale of government borrowing. Public debt across OECD nations has ballooned, driven by pandemic relief, defense spending commitments, and aging demographic entitlements.

Unlike the post-2008 era of quantitative easing, central banks are no longer soaking up supply to suppress yields. Bond vigilantes, once thought dormant, are demanding higher term premia to hold government paper.

This creates an acute political headache. Rising debt-servicing costs swallow tax revenues that could otherwise fund public services, while higher benchmark yields trickle into consumer mortgage and business lending rates.

For leaders facing elections across the rich world, the message from the bond market is clear: the era of free money is definitively over, and fiscal discipline can no longer be deferred.
