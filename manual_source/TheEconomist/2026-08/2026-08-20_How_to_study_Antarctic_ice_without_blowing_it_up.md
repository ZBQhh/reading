# How to study Antarctic ice without blowing it up

> **作者**: The Economist | **发布日期**: 2026-08-20 | **来源**: [TheEconomist](https://www.economist.com/science-and-technology/2026/08/19/how-to-study-antarctic-ice-without-blowing-it-up)

---

For decades, glaciologists seeking to map the deep structure of the Antarctic ice sheet relied on a crude but effective method: setting off dynamite.

Seismic waves generated by explosives travel through thousands of meters of solid ice, bouncing off bedrock and subglacial lakes before returning to surface geophones.

While scientifically valuable, detonating TNT on pristine polar ice is environmentally intrusive, logistically dangerous, and heavily restricted by international conservation protocols.

Now, a team of polar researchers has developed a non-destructive alternative: using the rhythmic thumping of specialized heavy tracked vehicles equipped with seismic vibrators.

These vibrating plates transmit controlled acoustic sweeps into the ice without fracturing the delicate firn layer or releasing chemical residues into ancient snow strata.

Early results from the West Antarctic Ice Sheet suggest the vibroseis technique produces higher-resolution subsurface imagery than legacy explosives, marking a vital step forward in modeling sea-level rise under climate change.
